To the Woman Who Has My Heart

My Love,

As I sit here writing this, all I can think about is how close we are to seeing each other again. Every day that passes brings me one step closer to you, and I honestly cannot wait. I miss you more than words can explain, and the thought of finally being able to hold you, look into your eyes, and feel your warmth again fills me with so much joy.

It still amazes me that very soon, I will stand beside you and promise you forever. Soon, you will not just be the person I love. You will be my wife, which means you are officially stuck with me for life. I hope you are fully prepared.

You have brought so much peace, warmth, and happiness into my life. The way you love me, care for me, and somehow tolerate all my nonsense makes me feel incredibly lucky. Being with you feels like home, and I cannot wait for us to begin this new chapter together.

I am looking forward to all of it. Seeing you walk towards me, holding your hand, building our home, travelling, laughing over silly things, arguing about what to eat, and facing every season of life together. More than anything, I am looking forward to finally being yours, completely and forever.

I pray God continues to bless us, guide us, and strengthen the life we are about to build. I promise to keep choosing you, loving you, protecting your heart, and doing my best to make you feel as cherished as you make me feel every day.

Thank you for being my peace, my happiness, and the greatest blessing in my life. I cannot wait to see you, hold you, annoy you in person again, and begin forever with you.

Yours always,
Kev ❤️
