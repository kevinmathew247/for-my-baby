A note for you my love.

You make me the happiest man in the world.

I do not think I will ever stop being grateful for the way you came into my life. You make me so happy in the most real and steady way. With you, I feel at peace. I never feel like I have to be anyone else. I can just be me, and somehow that version of me is happier, calmer, and more complete because of you.

I look forward to marrying you more than I can properly put into words. I look forward to waking up next to you everyday, laughing with you, spending ordinary days with you, building a home with you, and growing through every season of life together. The thought of forever with you does not scare me. It comforts me.

I want you to know this clearly: I will always do my level best to keep you happy, to take care of your heart, and to be the kind of husband you can be proud of. But more than that, I love how we are with each other — how we’re a little obsessed, a little clingy, always wanting to be close, always finding comfort in just holding onto each other. There’s something so special about the way we love, and I never want to lose that. I want to keep choosing you, every single day, for the rest of my life.

You are one of the best things that has ever happened to me. I love your heart, your presence, your warmth, and the way you make even simple moments feel special. If this is what love feels like with you, then I know I have found something I never want to lose.

Yours always,
Kev ❤️
